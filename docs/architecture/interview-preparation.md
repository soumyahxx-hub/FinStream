# Interview preparation guide

Be ready to explain stable event identity, why idempotency differs from exactly-once, KRaft versus ZooKeeper, Schema Registry evolution, event-time watermarks, checkpoints, why PostgreSQL is not the raw lake, dbt versus Spark, and why Airflow should not run a continuous stream. Demonstrate one measured replay with recovery/reconciliation evidence rather than presenting an unverified diagram as a finished production platform.
